My story

I believe that nobody's story can truly be theirs until they start writing it by themselves. I am writing this at 25 and just beginning the process of truly writing my own. Up until recently, my decisions, behaviors, and thoughts have been heavily influenced by my family and friends. When I started the process of becoming more independent and thinking for myself, I really started to realize what was important to me. It's not money, fame, or power like we as humans all innately strive for, but to redevelop my thought patterns, transcend out of the herd mentality (ubermensch) and to truly know / value myself to the core.

I believe in a world where people care for each other no matter what social class / race, and I believe that once I truly appreciate myself, I can then truly give back to people in the best way I see fit. Life to me is about the better than before life you leave behind for others, whether it is the wealth / opportunities you pass on or the personal relationships that shape people into better human beings. If all us focused on this instead of being a cog in the self-centered / ostentatious displays of the consumer economy, maybe the world would be a better place. I spend every day reading and thinking about these things, we'll see where my story takes me!
